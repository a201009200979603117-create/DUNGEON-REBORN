# 封面圖片資料夾

把 AI 生成好的封面圖片命名為 **`cover.png`** 放在這個資料夾（`images/cover/cover.png`），
主頁就會自動改用它當背景；沒有放的話會顯示預設的 `cover-placeholder.svg`。

- 建議尺寸：**1920 × 1080（16:9）** 以上
- 構圖重點：畫面**中央偏上要留暗、留空**，因為遊戲標題「DUNGEON REBORN」與按鈕會疊在上面
- 主頁已經加了由上到下的暗色漸層，圖片亮一點也沒關係

---

## AI 生成提示詞（英文，適用 Midjourney / DALL·E / Stable Diffusion / Leonardo）

```
Epic dark fantasy roguelike game key art, a lone hooded warrior seen from behind standing at the
entrance of an ancient underground dungeon, holding a glowing sword, a massive stone archway with
a blazing orange portal of light deep inside, hordes of glowing green slimes emerging from the
shadows on both sides, flickering wall torches, floating embers and sparks in the air, cracked
stone floor with golden runes, cinematic volumetric lighting, warm orange and gold vs deep purple
and teal shadows, dramatic atmosphere, highly detailed digital painting, painterly game cover
illustration, wide shot, symmetrical composition, dark empty space in the upper center for the
title text, no text, no logo, no watermark
```

Midjourney 可加參數：

```
--ar 16:9 --style raw --s 250 --v 6
```

Stable Diffusion 負面提示詞（Negative prompt）：

```
text, watermark, logo, signature, blurry, lowres, bad anatomy, extra limbs, deformed hands,
cropped, jpeg artifacts, oversaturated, bright daylight, cartoon, chibi
```
